Background

Outcome Memories was an asymmetrical survival horror game based on the old internet creepypasta "Sonic.exe"
(and a few other Sonic creepypastas). It was created as a passion project that eventually was cancelled
due to controversies and rude fans.
However, the game is still being updated, with the final batch being released sometime in the future

Author's Opinion

This game has been my addiction for the past several months. It's been a lot of fun, messing around with people
and getting to know others. The game is also just fun to play, despite the weird jank from the engine being Roblox.
Overall, it's just been a good time, despite all the controversy and bugs in the game

Features:

  • 9 Survivors, all with unique strengths and weaknesses
  • 4 EXEcutioners, each with different skills
  • 6 maps, from Green Hill to X's Castle
  • Cosmetics!
  • Emotes!
  • The Master Plan!

  • And more!
